# How to research an ETF
## The CDAA method (Companies Dollars and Asset Allocation)
### Companies
1. Find out who created the fund?
1. Find the propectus either from their website or just search it on the internet. Every fund is required to provide a prospectus by the SEC. From the documents and mostoften made easy on the website, find the following:
    1. How much is in the fund?
    1. How many companies are in the fund?
    1. What type of companies are in the fund?
    1. What are the allocations (percentage of fund a company has in a fund)
    1. What is the expense ratio? Use the expense ratio and do some calculations
