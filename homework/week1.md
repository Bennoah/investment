# Types of investment strategies

There are two types of investment strategies: active and passive

## Active investment

This is for seasoned investors who know how to research investment opportunities, and can evaluate the risk associated with investing in a stock. This requires time and attention to market shift initiators.

## Passive investors

This is ideal for beginners. It usually involves buying a stock, preferably an ETF (group of stock, and has a manager). The ETF manager ensures that the winning stock are maintained and the losing stocks are removed from the ETF.
